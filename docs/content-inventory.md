## Cross-check against the 27 Sep 2026 content pack

The pack’s inventory (41 pages, 733 posts) was compared with this concept. Section 13 of that inventory flags figures that must not be stated as current. On this site:

- The founder’s birth year is omitted. The pack records a 1910–2000 span in The Legend Endures and a 108th anniversary on 4 March 2024, which would imply 1916. The anniversary is described without the year.
- Staff strength of 1,000, the chairman’s age of 46, a “seven-and-a-half decade” history, Goa’s population of 1.5 million, the club page’s “fifth decade”, and the trust page’s aid of rupees five lakhs are not shown as current figures.
- Hindustan Foods stays in the menu because dempos.com still has the page, with a visible placeholder after the 2013 change of control.
- Emerging projects, the Ratanpar yard, and Devashri’s “under construction” list are labelled as the pages left them, not as 2026 status.
- Goa Carbon capacity is shown as both figures the site prints: 240,000 MT on the company page and 308,000 MT in the June 2024 gcarb+ note.
- Contact numbers 2225098 and 2228588 are printed without being labelled fax, because the contact page itself does not label them. Company pages call them fax.

People added from posts in the pack, with only the titles those posts use: Girija Dempo, Yatish Dempo, Anupam Misra, Sharmila Prabhu, and Dr. Krishna Gopal Rajanala.
